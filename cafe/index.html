Our Story

A little café on
an old street corner

Peninsula Coffee sits on a quiet corner of Harbor Road, inside a two-story wooden building. The ground floor is for the bar and our roaster. The second floor is for anyone who wants to stay all afternoon.

We roast our beans in small batches every week and bake our desserts fresh every morning, until they sell out. We are never in a hurry. We just hope that when you walk through the door, you slow down a little too.
